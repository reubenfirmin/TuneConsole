How the comparison works

Choosing a setup

After 14 days of history and 100 modeled tracks, the worker prepares a challenger with the same library, taste inputs and serving pipeline. Complete recommendation menus and Radio episodes keep their assigned setup. New windows share a random assignment for each UTC day.

A decision follows 28 days of exposure and one day for outcomes. It needs observed days in both setups, adequate observation coverage, a supported useful improvement, and separate checks for each used format. Correlated tracks stay within their episode and day. Uncertain comparisons keep the current model. Repeated attempts use a shared error budget.

Checking later listening

Visibility, choice, playback start and observed completion remain separate. Missing endings stay unknown. After a switch, weekly checks report coverage and sustained observed declines, with an option to restore the exact previous model. Later monitoring is observational.

Earlier first-play forecasts remain available as representation diagnostics. They do not establish whether a recommendation menu improved.
