How the comparison works

Choosing a setup

We predict which unplayed tracks in your library you’ll listen to later. After 14 days of history and 100 modeled tracks, each setup picks 20 tracks using only library and listening data from before a test period. We then check which tracks you actually played for the first time during that period. Later playlist edits are excluded.

A switch needs at least {{ s.min_trials }} comparable tracks across {{ s.min_windows }} periods, improvement in at least two periods, no worse period, and a paired statistical check adjusted for the number of alternatives. Small or uncertain differences keep the current setup.

Checking later listening

After selection, we save both setups’ lists of 20 predictions before new plays happen, then check them against your later listening. A score of 40% means 4 of 10 eligible tracks you went on to play were on that setup’s list. Only tracks both models knew, with no earlier play in your saved history, count.

Forecasts refresh during model updates, at most once a day. Plays only from app-generated playlists are excluded. Prediction accuracy doesn’t measure a change in enjoyment.
