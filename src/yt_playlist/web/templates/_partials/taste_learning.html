Recent & learning

Recommendations sets the balance of your picks: variety, genre preferences, and how strongly recent plays and likes count. This tab shows how your listening and feedback change those picks over time. The controls below set how quickly recent influence fades and how much evidence is needed to change a saved preference.

For example, playing more jazz can temporarily favor jazz. Continued listening can also raise its saved preference—the same value you can adjust in Recommendations’ Genres controls. Increasing Follow recent plays also makes plays build learning evidence faster.

Learning progress

The meters show evidence toward recommending each artist, genre or era more or less often. At 100%, its saved preference gets a small update and the meter builds again.
