How these counts work

Ratings on radio songs count too. These are retained inputs first recorded in the last 7 days, not a count of every rating action. Existing likes were discovered by sync; their original rating dates aren’t known. Likes can build lasting preferences; a dislike steers away from that song, not its whole genre.
