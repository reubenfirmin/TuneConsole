Richer music details · Last.fm

We look up song and artist tags on Last.fm and add their genre information to the music details used by your taste model, mixes and radio.

Last.fm is currently unavailable. Check its connection in Setup.

Get a Last.fm key: a code that lets TuneConsole look up music details. Setup shows where to get one and where to paste it.

Song and artist names are sent to Last.fm for lookups. TuneConsole doesn’t post your listening there.

Stories behind the records · DeepSeek

When you open an album, we can ask DeepSeek to write a short AI description from its public album details.

Album descriptions are waiting. Check the service status and request limit in Setup.

See the setup steps

Add a DeepSeek API key in Setup and choose a daily request limit. This service uses your own paid DeepSeek account; requests can incur charges.

Only public album details are sent: artist, album title and year. Your listening history stays here.
