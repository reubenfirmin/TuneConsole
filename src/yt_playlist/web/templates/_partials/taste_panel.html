How your picks take shape

TuneConsole combines several signals: track similarity, relationships between artists, and information about genre, era, and sound. Your playlists, listening history, and feedback help it work out which music fits your taste. Recent activity can shift that balance toward what you’re enjoying now.

These settings let you change that balance: give less-played genres more room, make recommendations follow recent listening more closely, or favor specific genres. If a week of jazz is crowding out the rest of your collection, for example, reducing recent-play influence gives your broader taste more say.

Your changes are saved immediately. You’ll see them the next time recommendations refresh. In Experiment, save a starting point and compare sample picks before and after an adjustment. Home and Discover each use the model differently; each control explains where it applies.
