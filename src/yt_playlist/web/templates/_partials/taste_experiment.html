Return to defaults

Resets all Taste Model settings, including Recent & learning, and removes saved genre, era, artist, and recommendation mix preferences. Your trained model, listening history, and feedback are kept. The music in this preview

The shortlist behind Home’s familiar-music mix: Taste matches and overlooked songs by artists you play often (Deep cuts). Until taste matching is ready, songs that share playlists with your favorites (Playlist neighbors) fill in.

This pool is the set of songs the mix can draw from. The final Home mix also applies genre choices, variety limits, and track ordering.

Settings that shape these picks

Main controls, Genres, and shared ranking settings under Settings by feature apply here. So do recent-listening influence and memory in Recent & learning, and Source weights in Listening choices.

Your library, listening, feedback, and trained model also shape these picks. Check other Home mixes, Discover searches, and cleanup in those features.
