Mood direction session · transient

Two of the four layers exist here as a direction in the embedding (learned from co-listening, not surface genre tags): session, this sitting on a {{ '%.0f'|format(tilt.halflife_h) }}h half-life, and transient, the last few weeks. Each is projected onto the same genre centroids, so the two bars are on one scale: signed alignments from -1 to +1 growing from the center, toward a sound (teal, right) or away from it (red, left). Playlist influence permanent

The recommender doesn't blur all your music into one average. It treats each playlist as a distinct sound and blends them, weighting each by how much you actually listen to it. So your heavy-rotation playlists steer your picks, while a rarely-played one (the road-trip-with-Dad playlist) barely counts and can't drag recommendations off-taste. Each bar is a playlist's share of that blend; hover for its dominant genre.
