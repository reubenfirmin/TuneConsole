The four layers

Your taste is modelled at four timescales at once, fastest to slowest. They do not all speak the same language, so each card below shows only the layers that genuinely exist on its axis - the rest would be invented.

  1. NOWhours Which of your taste modes you're playing right now. Too few plays to estimate a direction, so it's a share across modes, and it stays silent below {{ viz.modes.min_events }} plays rather than guess. See: Taste modes.
  2. SESSIONa day This sitting, fading on a {{ '%.0f'|format(viz.modes.session.halflife_h) }}h half-life. Both a mode balance and a direction in the embedding. See: Taste modes, Mood direction.
  3. TRANSIENTdays to weeks Recent plays, likes, dislikes, mood taps and skips, each fading on its own clock. Becomes a multiplier on every genre, era and artist. See: Genre families, Eras, Artists, Mood direction.
  4. PERMANENTno decay Your durable taste: weights graduated slowly from your history, the sliders you hold, and the embedding itself. See: everything below.

This view is read-only; adjust anything on the Controls tab.

Where each layer acts decision routing

The layers are not blended into one universal taste number. Each one acts at a particular decision seam; this is why the same play can inform several timescales without being counted twice inside one calculation.
