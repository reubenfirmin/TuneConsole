How recommendation layers work {# ── The stack, stated once ──────────────────────────────────────────────── #}

The four layers

Recommendations combine what you are playing now, this listening session, recent feedback, and saved preferences. Taste profile shows how these affect your music preferences; Recent & learning shows how they change over time.

  1. CURRENThours Which listening styles you are playing now. Requires at least {{ viz.modes.min_events }} plays with enough track information. See: Listening styles.
  2. SESSIONa day Music from this listening session. Its influence halves every {{ '%.0f'|format(viz.modes.session.halflife_h) }} hours. See: Listening styles, Recent sound.
  3. RECENTdays to weeks Recent plays, likes, dislikes, mood taps and skips, each fading on its own schedule. These raise or lower scores for genres, eras, and artists. See: Genre families, Eras, Artists, Recent sound.
  4. SAVEDlasting Preferences learned over time, your saved slider settings, and the trained model. Shown throughout Taste profile.

Read your preferences in Taste profile, adjust them in Recommendations, and control learning in Recent & learning.

What each layer changes

Each layer affects a different part of the recommendation process, such as choosing a listening style or ranking individual tracks.
