How recommendation layers work {# ── The stack, stated once ──────────────────────────────────────────────── #}

The four layers

Recommendations combine the current session, listening from earlier today, recent feedback, and lasting preferences.

  1. NOWactive session Playback and choices guide the active session. Inferred listening styles require at least {{ viz.modes.min_events }} distinct tracks with enough information. Manual steering applies immediately.
  2. TODAYlocal calendar day Listening across today's sessions. Its influence halves every {{ '%.0f'|format(viz.modes.session.halflife_h) }} hours and resets at local midnight. See Listening styles and Recent sound.
  3. RECENTdays to weeks Recent plays, likes, dislikes, mood taps and skips, each fading on its own schedule. These raise or lower scores for genres, eras, and artists. See Genre families, Eras, Artists, and Recent sound.
  4. PERMANENTlasting Preferences learned from sustained listening and deliberate actions across independent days. See Taste profile and Recent & learning.

Read your preferences in Taste profile, adjust them in Recommendations, and control learning in Recent & learning.

What each layer changes

Each layer affects a different part of the recommendation process, such as choosing a listening style or ranking individual tracks.
