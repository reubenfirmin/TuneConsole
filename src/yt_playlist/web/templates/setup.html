Pair the browser extension

Why an extension?

TuneConsole needs your library and listening history to create recommendations. Google's public playlist API can't read your listening history, so TuneConsole connects through an extension in your signed-in browser instead.

The extension makes YouTube Music requests in your browser and returns the results to TuneConsole over a local connection. Your session cookies stay in the browser; TuneConsole never receives or stores them.

What does this give access to?

Only your YouTube Music library, playlists, and listening history. The extension uses the sign-in your browser already has for YouTube Music, but your session cookie never leaves the browser: it is applied to each request inside the page, and only the response comes back to this app. The app never receives or stores your session cookie; it sends only the request (method, URL, body).

The connection is scoped to YouTube Music: the extension only signs requests to YouTube Music's own API and refuses anything else, so it cannot be used to reach Gmail, Drive, or other Google services. You can watch every request the app makes on the Network page (full details in the privacy policy). To revoke access, remove the extension or sign out of YouTube Music in the browser.

Install the TuneConsole extension and it connects automatically, there is nothing to paste. Keep a music.youtube.com tab open while signed in. The extension is recognized by its identity, so no other program can use this connection.

Import Spotify playlists and albums

Upload Spotify’s Account Data zip to recreate your playlists and save your albums in YouTube Music. TuneConsole matches songs and albums locally through your connected YouTube Music account and remembers imported items, so retrying the same export will not create duplicates.

How to export
  • Open Spotify’s Account Privacy page.
  • Under Download your data, select Account data and submit the request. Spotify may ask you to confirm it by email.
  • Be prepared to wait several days. Spotify does not create the download immediately; it emails you when the Account Data zip is finally ready.
  • Download that zip and upload it here as-is. It should contain files such as Playlist1.json and YourLibrary.json. Extended Streaming History and Technical Log Information are different packages.

Import Spotify listening history

Upload your Spotify Extended Streaming History to fold past Spotify listening into TuneConsole’s taste model. Everything is processed locally on this machine: the file never leaves it.

How to export
  • Open Spotify’s Account Privacy page.
  • Under Download your data, select both Account data and Extended streaming history, then request the data. Spotify may ask you to confirm the request by email, and the download emails can take several days to arrive.
  • When Spotify emails that each package is ready, download and keep both zip files. Account data contains your playlists, saved songs, albums, and artists; Extended streaming history contains your past plays.
  • Upload the Extended streaming history zip here as-is. Use the separate form above for the Account Data zip.

Import YouTube history (Google Takeout)

The live sync only sees new plays going forward. Upload your Google Takeout watch-history.json to backfill your full listening history. Everything is processed locally on this machine: the file never leaves it.

How to export
  • Go to takeout.google.com
  • Click Deselect all
  • Select only YouTube and YouTube Music
  • Click All YouTube data included. If it does not appear, try refreshing the page and/or temporarily disabling your ad blocker—Google Takeout can be flaky.
  • Deselect all, then select history only
  • Click Multiple formats, scroll down to History, click the menu at the right where it says HTML, and choose JSON
  • Export, then upload the downloaded takeout-*.zip here as is (or the watch-history.json from inside it)

Your identities

Add one row for each YouTube identity. Choose a main identity to receive playlists merged from multiple identities. Leave Brand account ID blank for your personal account. Changes save and check automatically.

Find your Brand account ID

  1. Click Open Google Brand Accounts {{ arrow_icon('external') }}. Use the Google account signed in to YouTube Music.
  2. Under Your Brand Accounts, click the identity’s name. If it is missing, use your profile picture at the top right to switch Google accounts.
  3. In the browser’s address bar, copy only the number after /b/. Example address: myaccount.google.com/b/123456789012345678901/
  4. Paste your number into Brand account ID and enter a Label. Changes save and check automatically when you leave the field.

For your personal account, leave Brand account ID blank.
