Privacy policy

Short and sweet:

  • We don't capture your data. Your sign-in, playlists, and library stay on your machine.
  • We don't capture analytics. No tracking, no telemetry, no phoning home.
  • The app stores its data locally and talks to YouTube and the music services listed on the Network page.
  • If you enable DeepSeek album descriptions, the artist, album title and release year are sent to DeepSeek using your API key. Your listening history and taste profile stay local. Descriptions are saved locally and reused; you can pause generation or remove the saved key in Settings.
  • YouTube Music authentication stays in your browser. TuneConsole does not store Google session cookies or OAuth tokens.
