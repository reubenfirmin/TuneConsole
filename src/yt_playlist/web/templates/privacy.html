Privacy policy

Short and sweet:

  • We don't capture your data. Your sign-in, playlists, and library stay on your machine.
  • We don't capture analytics. No tracking, no telemetry, no phoning home.
  • This app is entirely local. It only ever talks to YouTube (and the music-metadata services you can see on the Network page) to do its job.
  • If you connect Playlist dates, a read-only Google authorization is stored in a private file on this computer. It is used only to read YouTube channel and playlist metadata. Disconnecting removes that account's saved authorization; dates already read are kept.
