TuneConsole's Relax consensus contains frozen ridge coefficients and preprocessing
statistics from #617. It distributes no source audio or per-song annotations.
The fixed blend uses Emotify (50%), Soundtracks (25%), ManyMusic (12.5%, split
equally between natural and generated recordings), and Memo2496 (12.5%).

Sources and retained terms:

- [Emotify](https://www2.projects.science.uu.nl/memotion/emotifydata/): 400
  supplied excerpts with native felt-emotion annotations. Retain the publisher's
  source terms; this export does not establish separate distribution clearance.
- [Soundtracks](https://osf.io/p6vkg/): 470 retained excerpts from the supplied
  sets, preserving native energy, tension and tenderness. Retain the publisher's
  source terms; overlapping sets are not independent datasets.
- [ManyMusic-Stim v2](https://zenodo.org/records/23647077): 188 natural and 95
  generated recordings. The [publisher](https://manymusic.net/stim/) specifies
  CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 for generated audio, metadata and human ratings. Natural
  recordings retain their individual Jamendo licences.
- [Memo2496](https://figshare.com/articles/dataset/Memo2496/25827034): the 63
  audio-backed pilot recordings; the source record lists CC BY 4.0.

The research export's source-terms status is retained. This notice does not
establish distribution clearance for this derived artifact or relicense the
source datasets under TuneConsole's MIT software license. The frozen plan and
source model hashes are recorded in the artifact.
