The Release model is TuneConsole's frozen ridge predictor of the human
`aggressive` attribute from MTG-Jamendo, trained on 187 protected-source rows.
Only coefficients and preprocessing statistics are bundled; no source audio or
per-song annotations are distributed here.

Source: Music Technology Group, MTG-Jamendo Dataset, publisher commit
`cafd8e20c265ed84f1e61f1c875327971f43a62f`.
Bogdanov, Won, Tovstogan, Porter and Serra, *The MTG-Jamendo Dataset for Automatic
Music Tagging*, ICML 2019 Machine Learning for Music Discovery Workshop.
