TuneConsole's Energize consensus contains seven frozen native ridge heads from
#616's no-joy variant. It distributes no source audio or per-song annotations.
The fixed blend is 50% source-balanced arousal, 25% Soundtracks energy and 25%
Emotify power. Joy, CAL500 and the learned exercise mappings are not included.

Sources and retained terms:

- [DEAM](https://cvml.unige.ch/databases/DEAM/): 1,800 retained recordings,
  native arousal. Retain the original publisher's terms.
- [PMEmo](https://github.com/HuiZhangDB/PMEmo): 766 retained choruses,
  native arousal. Retain the original publisher's terms.
- [Soundtracks](https://osf.io/p6vkg/): 470 retained excerpts, native energy.
  Retain source terms; overlapping sets are not independent.
- [Emotify](https://www2.projects.science.uu.nl/memotion/emotifydata/): 400
  supplied excerpts with native felt-power annotations. Retain source terms.
- [ManyMusic-Stim v2](https://zenodo.org/records/23647077): 188 natural and
  95 generated recordings, native arousal. The [publisher](https://manymusic.net/stim/)
  specifies CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 for generated audio, metadata and human ratings.
  Natural recordings retain their individual Jamendo licences.
- [Memo2496](https://figshare.com/articles/dataset/Memo2496/25827034): 63
  retained pilot recordings, native arousal; the source record lists CC BY 4.0.

Arousal divides equally between DEAM, PMEmo, ManyMusic and Memo, with equal
natural/generated shares within ManyMusic. These are 3,782 retained source
rows, not a claim of 3,782 distinct independent identities.

The original research source-terms status is retained in the artifact, including
the provenance of omitted diagnostic sources. This notice does not establish
distribution clearance or relicense datasets under TuneConsole's MIT software
license. User adoption favors broader labeled-source coverage and accepts the
reported losses; it does not change the original research retain recommendation.
