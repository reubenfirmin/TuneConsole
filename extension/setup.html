Checking the app connection…

This extension connects YouTube Music to the TuneConsole app on this computer. Install and open the app for mixes, library tools, and listening history.

Already installed? Start TuneConsole from your applications menu.

Get the app
Open TuneConsole

Keep a signed-in YouTube Music tab open. It connects automatically.

YouTube Music works normally without the app. Listening history is saved only while TuneConsole is running.
