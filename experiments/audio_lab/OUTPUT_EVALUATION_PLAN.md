runtime and seven model artifacts. Keep the enlarged current local annotation

* Metadata: retained metadata-only profile dimensions plus original provider

  propagation or metadata-derived musical dimensions. Missing or partial audio

  eligibility, genre restrictions, taste and diversity in every arm.

  captured-audio inference taking precedence where usable. Apply the production
  propagation policy, with one original recording vote and no self-vote.
