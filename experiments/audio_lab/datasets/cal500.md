# CAL500 — Computer Audition Lab 500

Source and downloads: [UC San Diego CAL500 release](http://calab1.ucsd.edu/~datasets/cal500/).
Annotations: [CAL500_noAudioFeatures.tar.gz](http://calab1.ucsd.edu/~datasets/cal500/cal500data/CAL500_noAudioFeatures.tar.gz).
Format: [publisher's dataset description](http://calab1.ucsd.edu/~datasets/cal500/details_cal500.txt).
Terms: [publisher's use conditions](http://calab1.ucsd.edu/~datasets/cal500/use_cal500.txt).

CAL500 is a collection of Western popular music annotated by listeners for
instruments, vocals, genre, emotions, song qualities and listening activities.
The annotation release has 502 song rows and 174 retained semantic labels,
including 15 activities such as Reading, Studying, Driving and Exercising.
It supplies raw listener responses, aggregate weights, binary labels and song
names; the audio release supplies 500 recordings.

TuneConsole has 496 recordings long enough for ten-second inputs, or 494 after
protected recording exclusions. Focus uses the original listener checkbox
fractions for Reading and Studying. The published activity matrix discards
single-listener endorsements and retains fractions when at least two listeners
select an activity; this differs from the README's stated 80% consensus rule.
Keep the raw-vote and published targets distinct. Unselected checkboxes in
completed responses are negative; explicit unknown answers remain missing.

Activity labels express whole-song listening suitability. They do not measure
improved concentration, and short excerpts need not represent the whole song.
Listener identity is mostly absent, limiting checks of listener dependence.
