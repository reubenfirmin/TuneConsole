Local labels are acceptable alternatives supplied by the developer. Preserve
original vocabulary, cleared/missing answers, sample IDs and feedback-time
model identity. A selected vibe is positive; an unselected vibe is unknown.
Teacher predictions and propagated descendants are not additional human votes.

Metadata-transfer evaluation resolves feedback through playback video identities
and recording redirects, including labels without captured samples. Preserve
supersession and the latest empty/withdrawn judgment across alternate uploads;
do not revive an earlier positive. Reserve eligibility means absence from the
supplied historical identity snapshots, not proof of no earlier exposure.
Evaluation excludes every human target's original measurements from fitting. Current
original-only coverage is 3,441 recordings per vibe: 3,201 provider and 240
captured-audio measurements; historical and retracted measurements are excluded.

Frozen lineage deduplicates original recording contributors and excludes
descendants as new measurements. Propagation retraction matched a full rebuild;
agreement with withheld model outputs is consistency evidence, not human accuracy.
Provider-only, inherited, legacy heuristic and compatible specialist scores have
different provenance and scales. A model hash on fallback does not prove learned
inference. Complete capture alone does not establish embedding compatibility.

Oct 30, #585: twelve balanced library/discovery evidence cases, seventeen bounded
ReccoBeats requests, zero verified recording additions or new fields. Six cases
were ambiguous and six lacked acceptable matches. Candidate title/artist matches
without accepted shared identifiers and duration do not establish identity;
different provider score ranges do not establish equivalent attributes.
GetSongBPM lacked an application key/attribution setting and was not measured.
Do not adopt either provider from this pilot; no broad name-only ingestion.

The evidence/provenance audit (#586) checked stored source versions, corrections,
