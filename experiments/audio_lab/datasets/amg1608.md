# AMG1608

Source: [author's dataset page](https://amg1608.blogspot.com/).
Acquired copy: [release mirror at the recorded revision](https://github.com/loichan-tw/AMG1608_release/tree/16ce26147f509e712c70cef99dc7dd0dfa912702).

AMG1608 contains 1,608 song identities and sparse whole-song valence/arousal
ratings from 665 listeners. Coordinates use a nominal −1 to 1 scale, with at
least 15 complete rating pairs per song. The release includes song metadata,
individual ratings and precomputed acoustic descriptors in a split MAT archive.
Missing ratings are unknown, rather than neutral coordinates. Six slightly
out-of-range individual coordinates are retained; song means remain in range.

TuneConsole uses an intersection of 404 existing audio excerpts in 310 connected
groups, matched by normalized artist/title. These excerpts come from other
acquired collections. This subset does not reproduce the original recording
versions, rated intervals or benchmark split. The supplied acoustic descriptors
are separate from features extracted by TuneConsole.
