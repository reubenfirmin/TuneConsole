# Moods and Activities

Source: [UK Data Service ReShare release](https://reshare.ukdataservice.ac.uk/852024/).
Downloads: [Data.zip](https://reshare.ukdataservice.ac.uk/852024/1/Data.zip)
and [publisher's codebook](https://reshare.ukdataservice.ac.uk/852024/3/ReadMe.txt).

This music-tagging study contains 5,129 track records, 28,550 listened-example
tagging rows and 8,681 track-nomination rows, plus listener background,
word/activity associations and consistency checks. Track metadata retains
7digital recording, artist and release IDs. No audio is supplied.

Listened-example responses indicate which moods the clip expresses and which
activities fit it. Activity checkboxes use 1 for checked and 0 for unchecked;
mood terms omitted from a question remain missing. The `intellectual` category
combines studying, reading, writing and desk work. Nominations and associations
with mood words are different tasks from judgments made while hearing a clip.

TuneConsole's annotation-only subset combines repeated listener/song responses
into 28,524 pairs from 1,604 listeners, retaining response fractions. The 209
normalized artist/title matches against existing audio do not verify recording
versions or rated windows and supply no new audio benchmark. Activity endorsement
is a suitability judgment, rather than a measured concentration benefit.
