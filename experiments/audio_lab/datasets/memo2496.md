# Memo2496 — Music Emotion Dataset with 2496 Songs

Source and versioned files: [publisher's Figshare release](https://figshare.com/articles/dataset/Memo2496/25827034).
Audio: [MusicRawData.zip](https://ndownloader.figshare.com/files/52292060).
Mean trajectories: [valence](https://ndownloader.figshare.com/files/52358369)
and [arousal](https://ndownloader.figshare.com/files/52358360).

Memo2496 contains 2,496 instrumental recordings with expert valence/arousal
trajectories. The release supplies MP3 audio, mean and standard-deviation
trajectories, individual annotation data, source metadata and acoustic features.
Track IDs link these files. Artist names are removed, so recording-level
partitions cannot establish unseen-artist evaluation. The publisher lists
CC BY 4.0.

TuneConsole uses two separate subsets: a 63-recording development pilot with
complete labels, and 120 evaluation recordings from a reserved selection of 128.
Eight reserved recordings lack the required duration and are excluded without
padding or replacement. Both subsets use the interval from 15 to 75 seconds,
represented by six ten-second chunks. An identical-audio pair in the evaluation
subset shares one component, leaving 119 components. Both subsets have been
evaluated; neither is an unused confirmation reserve.

The remaining release is not part of these subsets. Selective archive downloads
verify member CRC/SHA256; the whole audio archive checksum is unverified.
